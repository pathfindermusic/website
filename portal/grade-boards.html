← Grading report
Print-ready wall boards built from the Portal's grading records. Grade sheets (A4) show where every active student is today — one sheet per grade, with a NEW tag on anyone who moved up in the chosen month. The Congratulations board (A3) lists everyone who achieved a new grade in the chosen month; it opens on last month, so on the 1st just open this page and print. In the print dialog choose paper size A4 or A3 to match the tab, set margins to None and tick Background graphics.
