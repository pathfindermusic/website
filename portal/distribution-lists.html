Distribution Lists
Bcc-ready address lists to copy into Gmail — for threads that need real back-and-forth or an attachment, not the one-way Notifications sends
Paste these into Gmail's Bcc field, not To or Cc. Put your own studio address in To. Bcc keeps every family's email address private from every other family, and stops a "reply all" from blasting the whole list — replies land only with you, which is the point of using Gmail for this instead of Notifications. One address per student (their login email, else their own contact email, else a parent's — whichever is on file first). Teachers are listed by their login email.
Who should be on this list?
Same audiences as Notifications. The list builds as soon as you choose.
