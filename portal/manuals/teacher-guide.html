Getting started

Sign in at pathfindermusiclessons.com.au/portal/login.html with the email and password your studio set up for you. If this is your first time logging in, you'll be asked to set your own password before you can go any further.

As a teacher your sidebar has just three items: My Schedule (your landing page), My Students and My Tasks. You won't see the Front Desk, Curriculum, Reports or Super User sections admins use — those aren't hidden by accident, they simply aren't part of the teacher role.

My Schedule

Your personal weekly lesson calendar. At the top, a stats row shows Today's lessons, This week, My students, Unmarked (lessons that have ended but still need attendance recorded), and My tasks. If you have open tasks, an "Outstanding tasks" strip appears above your calendar listing the nearest ones.

The schedule shows one week at a time — use ‹ Today › to move between weeks. Each lesson card shows the time, student (or the group's members), instrument, and either a grade pill or a skill-level pill for that student.

Marking attendance

  • For a private lesson, use the inline dropdown on the lesson card: Present, Absent — No Credit, Absent — Notice Given, or Teacher Cancelled.
  • For a group lesson, click "Mark attendance" to open a modal listing every student — mark each one the same way. A student left unmarked is simply skipped, so double-check everyone's been set before you close the modal.
  • Once every student on a lesson is marked, it's automatically finalised — there's no separate save step for that.

Writing a lesson note

Click + Add Note (or "Edit Note" if one already exists) on a lesson to write practice notes for the student, optionally attach a Google Drive link, and pick teaching materials from the Artefact Library — filterable by grade, and you can select more than one. If the library is missing something you used, + Add your own lets you add it on the spot; it's attached to this lesson right away, but stays invisible to other teachers until an admin reviews and approves it.

Saving a note emails the student automatically

As soon as you save, the student (and their parent, where applicable) receives an email with your note and any materials you picked — there's no extra "send" step, so make sure the note reads the way you want a family to see it before saving.

Clicking anywhere else on a lesson opens a read-only view showing the previous lesson's note for context, plus the full details of the current one.

Covering for another teacher

If you're covering someone else's lesson, it will show on your schedule as "Covering for [teacher]," and on their own schedule as covered by you. You can view notes from earlier weeks and mark attendance for it exactly like your own lessons.

There's no day view or list view — only the week view — and you can't reschedule, cancel, or edit a lesson's time, duration or roster from here. If a lesson needs to move, contact your studio admin.

My Students

Your own roster, drawn from your active lesson bookings. Each student shows their enrolment status, a grade or skill-level pill per instrument they take with you, and a preview of your most recent lesson note for them.

  • Record grade (for graded instruments) opens a modal to log a new Body-of-Knowledge grade with an achieved date and an optional note. This always adds to the student's grade history rather than overwriting it, so nothing is lost by recording progress regularly.
  • Edit skill level (for the rare instrument outside the graded list) adjusts a 0–8 slider instead.
  • Message Student lets you email a student directly — pick which of your studios to send from, write a subject and message. The reply goes to that studio's inbox, not to you personally.
You won't see contact details here

Phone numbers, emails and guardian details aren't shown on this page by design — reaching a student is done entirely through "Message Student," which handles the address for you.

This page only shows students from your current, active lesson bookings — a one-off guest who sat in on a single lesson of yours won't appear here, even though they'll still show on that lesson in My Schedule.

My Tasks

A read-only list of things the studio has raised about you — it's not a general to-do list, and you can't create tasks here.

  • Tiles at the top let you jump to Overdue, Due today, Next 7 days, or All open.
  • Opening a task shows its full detail, but the fields themselves are read-only for you.
  • The only action available is Reply to the studio — type a message and send it. This flags the task as "replied" on the admin side, letting them know you've responded, and adds your message to the task's log.
You can reply, but only an admin can close a task

Marking a task done, cancelling it, or reassigning it is deliberately kept with the studio — your reply is how you let them know where things stand, and they take it from there.

Changing your password

Use the 🔑 Password link in the top bar at any time. If you're logging in with a temporary password for the first time, you'll be taken here automatically and can't skip it.
