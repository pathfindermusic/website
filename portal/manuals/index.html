Pathfinder Portal — How-To Guide
Everything you need to get comfortable with the new Student Portal.

Welcome to the Pathfinder Music Lessons Student Portal. This is a system built specifically for our studios, running alongside our existing tools — the website, Zoho CRM, and MyMusicStaff — while we learn it, test it, and decide together when it's ready to take over fully.

This guide won't change anything about how you use the website, Zoho, or MyMusicStaff. It only explains the Portal: what it does, who sees what, and how to do the things you'll need to do day to day. Pick the guide that matches your role below, or start with the System Overview if you want the bigger picture first.
