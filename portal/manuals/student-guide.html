Signing in

Go to pathfindermusiclessons.com.au/portal/login.html and sign in with your email address and password. There's a Forgot password? link if you need to reset it — enter your email and a reset link will be emailed to you.

If your studio has just set up your account, you'll be given a temporary password and asked to choose your own the first time you log in — you can't skip this step.

Account not working?

If you see a message saying your account isn't fully set up, or is no longer active, contact your studio directly — this usually means your enrolment status needs to be checked on their end, rather than anything wrong with your password.

My Lessons

Your landing page after signing in. At the top, a summary shows your next lesson, how many lessons you have this week, your attendance rate, and how many practice notes have been added in the last week.

Below that, your schedule is shown one week at a time — use ‹ Today › to move between weeks. Each lesson shows its time, instrument, teacher and studio, with an "Online lesson" tag and a Join online button if it's a virtual lesson, and a "Cancelled" tag if it's been cancelled.

If your teacher has left a practice note or attached materials for a lesson, it appears directly underneath that lesson — including a "View shared file" link and a list of materials grouped by Repertoire, Technique and Musical Knowledge.

This page is for viewing only — there's no button here to request a reschedule or send a message to your teacher. For that, contact your studio directly.

Practice Notes

A running feed of every practice note your teacher has written after a lesson, most recent first — instrument, teacher, date, the note itself, and any materials or shared files that go with it. If you haven't had any notes yet, this simply shows "No practice notes yet."

Attendance

Three summary numbers — lessons attended, lessons held, and your attendance rate — followed by a list of your recent lessons with their attendance status. Only lessons that have actually taken place are counted; a lesson still to come is never shown as missed, and a cancelled lesson isn't counted against you.

My Progress

Shows the skill levels your teacher has recorded for you, instrument by instrument, using the same Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced grade ladder used across the whole studio. If you haven't been graded yet for an instrument, it will show "Not yet graded" rather than a blank.

  • Grade history lists every grade you've achieved over time, with the date and any notes your teacher added — this only appears once you have at least one recorded grade.
  • Materials you've covered lists everything from the Artefact Library that's been used in your lessons, grouped by Repertoire, Technique and Musical Knowledge.

This page is informational only — your grade is set by your teacher after a lesson, not something you record yourself.

Multiple children on one login

If your family has more than one child taking lessons and they share a single login, a row of buttons will appear above the summary numbers letting you switch between children — this only shows up when there's more than one child on the account, so most students will never see it. Switching reloads the schedule and stats for whichever child is selected, and the Portal remembers your choice for the rest of your visit.

If your family has more than one instrument across one or more children, each instrument simply shows as its own row wherever relevant — there's no separate switch needed for that.

Changing your password

Use the 🔑 Password link at the top of the page at any time. If you're logging in with a temporary password for the first time, you'll be taken here automatically before you can see your dashboard.
