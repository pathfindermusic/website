<h2 id="signing-in">Signing in</h2>
<p>
Go to <code>pathfindermusiclessons.com.au/portal/login.html</code> and sign
in with your email address and password. There's a
<strong>Forgot password?</strong> link if you need to reset it — enter your
email and a reset link will be emailed to you.
</p>
<p>
If your studio has just set up your account, you'll be given a temporary
password and asked to choose your own the first time you log in — you can't
skip this step.
</p>
<div class="callout callout-tip">
<strong>Account not working?</strong>
<p>
If you see a message saying your account isn't fully set up, or is no
longer active, contact your studio directly — this usually means your
enrolment status needs to be checked on their end, rather than anything
wrong with your password.
</p>
</div>

<h2 id="lessons">My Lessons</h2>
<p>
Your landing page after signing in. At the top, a summary shows your next
lesson, how many lessons you have this week, your attendance rate, and how
many practice notes have been added in the last week.
</p>
<p>
Below that, your schedule is shown one week at a time — use
<code>‹ Today ›</code> to move between weeks. Each lesson shows its time,
instrument, teacher and studio, with an "Online lesson" tag and a
<strong>Join online</strong> button if it's a virtual lesson, and a
"Cancelled" tag if it's been cancelled.
</p>
<p>
If the studio left a message when a lesson was cancelled, it shows under
that lesson as <strong>Note from the studio</strong>. If your teacher has
since sent a recorded lesson or other materials in its place, you'll see
that note and its "View shared file" link there too (and in your email).
</p>
<p>
If your teacher has left a practice note or attached materials for a
lesson, it appears directly underneath that lesson — including a "View
shared file" link and a list of materials grouped by Repertoire, Technique
and Musical Knowledge.
</p>
<p>
This page is for viewing only — there's no button here to request a
reschedule or send a message to your teacher. For that, contact your studio
directly.
</p>

<h2 id="notes">Practice Notes</h2>
<p>
A running feed of every practice note your teacher has written after a
lesson, most recent first — instrument, teacher, date, the note itself, and
any materials or shared files that go with it. If you haven't had any notes
yet, this simply shows "No practice notes yet."
</p>

<h2 id="attendance">Attendance</h2>
<p>
Three summary numbers — lessons attended, lessons held, and your attendance
rate — followed by a list of your recent lessons with their attendance
status. Only lessons that have actually taken place are counted; a lesson
still to come is never shown as missed, and a cancelled lesson isn't
counted against you.
</p>

<h2 id="progress">My Progress</h2>
<p>
Shows the skill levels your teacher has recorded for you, instrument by
instrument, using the same Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced
grade ladder used across the whole studio. If you haven't been graded yet
for an instrument, it will show "Not yet graded" rather than a blank.
</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Grade history</strong> lists every grade you've achieved over
time, with the date and any notes your teacher added — this only appears
once you have at least one recorded grade.</li>
<li><strong>Materials you've covered</strong> lists everything from the
Artefact Library that's been used in your lessons, grouped by
Repertoire, Technique and Musical Knowledge.</li>
</ul>
<p>
This page is informational only — your grade is set by your teacher after a
lesson, not something you record yourself.
</p>

<h2 id="events">Concerts and events</h2>
<p>
When the studio holds a concert, a card called <strong>Upcoming events — book
your performance</strong> appears at the top of your dashboard (and you'll
usually get an email with a link too). <strong>Events</strong> in the sidebar
lists everything on offer.
</p>
<p><strong>To book a performance:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Press <strong>Book now</strong>, then <strong>＋ Book a performance</strong>.
If you have more than one child on your login, choose who is performing.</li>
<li>Choose the <strong>instrument</strong> (or type a performance type such as
Voice, or choose <strong>Band</strong>) — you can book <strong>one performance
per instrument</strong>, so a student who plays guitar and is also in a band
can make two bookings. An instrument you've already booked shows as
“already booked”. Then choose the <strong>teacher</strong> — the teacher list follows the
instrument — then type the <strong>name of the piece</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose the <strong>accompaniment</strong>: your teacher, a peer (type their
name), a backing track, or none.</li>
<li>Choose up to <strong>three preferred time blocks</strong>. Only blocks with
room are offered. You'll be placed in the first one that still has a place and
told straight away which block and roughly what time. Add any comments for the
studio at the bottom.</li>
</ul>
<p>
Under <strong>My performances</strong> you can <strong>Edit</strong> or
<strong>Cancel booking</strong> until bookings close (inside Edit there is also a
red <strong>Cancel this booking</strong> button). Below that is the
programme so far: it updates by itself, your own performances are highlighted,
and other students appear only as a first name and last initial. The times
shown are approximate — please arrive at the start of your block. If your
preferred blocks are all full, you'll be asked to choose again; if bookings
have closed or you need a change, contact the studio.
</p>

<h2 id="family">Multiple children on one login</h2>
<p>
If your family has more than one child taking lessons and they share a
single login, a row of buttons will appear above the summary numbers
letting you switch between children — this only shows up when there's more
than one child on the account, so most students will never see it.
Switching reloads the schedule and stats for whichever child is selected,
and the Portal remembers your choice for the rest of your visit.
</p>
<p>
If your family has more than one instrument across one or more children,
each instrument simply shows as its own row wherever relevant — there's no
separate switch needed for that.
</p>

<h2 id="password">Changing your password</h2>
<p>
Use the <strong>🔑 Password</strong> link at the top of the page at any
time. If you're logging in with a temporary password for the first time,
you'll be taken here automatically before you can see your dashboard.
</p>
