The Perfect Gift for Any Music Lover

Music lesson gift vouchers are the perfect way to not only treat someone special to an experience they'll love, but to teach them a skill that will bring them joy for years to come.

In these lessons, your lucky gift recipient will:

  • Play songs they love — from the very first lesson
  • Learn the fundamentals of playing an instrument or singing
  • Grow their confidence in a friendly, expert environment
  • Realise their dream of learning to play music!

These lessons take place at Pathfinder Music Lessons, located in Kilsyth & Ringwood, Victoria.

How it works

  1. Choose a voucher and tell us who it's for.
  2. Pay securely by card on eWAY's payment page.
  3. We confirm your payment and email the voucher as a PDF — to you, and to the recipient if you gave us their address.
  4. The recipient books their lessons by emailing the studio of their choice and quoting the voucher number.

About the Teachers

Expert Teachers, Real Results

All of the teachers at Pathfinder Music Lessons have undertaken tertiary music qualifications (and hold valid Working with Children checks) and work professionally as performing musicians and teachers.

Passionate about giving musicians the ultimate learning experience that is fun, thorough and exciting, the Pathfinder team have helped thousands of new musicians reach their musical goals.
